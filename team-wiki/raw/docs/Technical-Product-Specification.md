# 1. Scope & Purpose

This Technical Product Specification (TPS) defines the engineering, mechanical, electrical, acoustic, and firmware requirements for the ZenaQuiet product family — ZenaTech's enterprise acoustic intelligence platform for high-density communication environments. The document is the contractual reference for ODM partners, internal engineering teams, ITAR-compliant manufacturing partners, and regulatory filings.

**Revision 1.3** adds Voice Lock Roster — a multi-voiceprint floor-level cancellation capability that extends single-speaker target extraction to subtractive identification across the full enrolled roster of voices on a floor (up to 64 voiceprints per coordinated zone). The Pro unit NPU spec is upgraded from ≥12 TOPS to ≥20 TOPS (Qualcomm QCS8550) to support multi-voiceprint inference; this raises BOM by \~$20 and compresses Pro gross margin from 68% to \~61% at the unchanged $299 ASP. Voice Lock Roster requires completion of biometric consent and federated enrollment infrastructure (Dentons engagement) as a hard dependency for Y1 GA. Two additional patents (#15, #16) added to the filing schedule.

**Revision 1.2** added ZenaHeadset Quiet (SKU #7, $449) as a Year-1 hero SKU with magnetic detachable acoustic arc and dual-path Voice Lock.

**Revision 1.1** added Section 14 — Zena AI Voice Lock biometric bidirectional voice isolation.

**Revision 1.0** locked the Halo Arc design and Beacon as discrete SKU.

## 1.3 Excerpt from Product Brief V5, Section 3.2

ZenaQuiet uses a **four-layer acoustic stack**. **No single technique creates a true silence bubble in open air — that physics does not exist commercially today** — but the layered approach delivers measurable, demonstrable suppression that the customer on the line and the agent at the seat both notice immediately.

# 3. ZenaQuiet Pro — Desk-Mounted Workstation Unit

### Original Specification

Twin-panel acoustic suppression system with:

1. Directional speaker arrays
2. Beam-forming microphone array
3. Integrated DSP.

Sits at the agent's workstation. Creates a bidirectional crosstalk-suppression zone spanning approximately 120° in front of the agent with effective performance to a 6-foot radius.

### High-Level Targets for PRO

_(from Zena Quiet Product Brief V5, Section 3)_:

1. Creates a personal quiet zone around each agent's workstation
2. Sound generated by the agent is contained at the workstation boundary
3. Sound from neighbors, the floor, and HVAC is suppressed at the agent's ear plane
4. The customer on the line hears only the agent
5. The agent hears only the call
6. Every deployed seat reports anonymized acoustic telemetry into the ZenaTech enterprise dashboard

### Three Things We Must Decide Before Engineering Starts

- **Hire audio specialists or partner with an acoustic consultancy.**

  Without at least one senior acoustic/audio DSP engineer, Layer 1 and Layer 2 cannot be built safely. The current team cannot self-teach their way through parametric arrays and ANC in 8 months while simultaneously delivering hardware.

- **Validate the market before building the product.**

  The BPO strategy came from an AI. Before spending 8 months building, spend 4 weeks interviewing 10 real BPO operations managers. Confirm the problem is real and the price point is credible. This de-risks the entire investment.

- **Redefine Q2 2027 as a proof-of-concept demo, not a commercial launch.**

  A working USB audio device with basic noise suppression, a passive Halo arc, and a credible TSE roadmap is a fundable, demo-able milestone. Calling it a commercial launch sets expectations the team cannot meet.

**Critical Point for Engineers — Zena AI Cloud Denoise Function**

Zena AI Cloud Denoise is positioned as a _fallback_ — but for this team:

- On-device TSE does not exist yet
- Cloud infrastructure does not exist yet
- The team has no ML deployment experience

**Cloud denoise is therefore not a fallback — it is a second major AI engineering workstream running in parallel with on-device TSE.** Both require ML audio engineers, which the team does not currently have. The CTO must treat these as two separate hiring and investment decisions, not one feature with a fallback.

# 14. Zena AI Voice Lock — Biometric Bidirectional Voice Isolation

> **Voice Lock** is the flagship intelligent capability of the ZenaQuiet platform: a biometric speaker-recognition system that performs bidirectional target-speaker extraction (TSE) on both the outbound and inbound audio channels of an agent's call.
>
> The system is trained on a 12-second voice sample enrolled at agent onboarding; from that point forward, the agent's voice is the only voice the customer hears on the outbound channel, and the customer's voice is the only voice the agent hears on the inbound channel.
>
> All other agents on the floor are eliminated from both channels in real time.

_Voice Lock is the engineering implementation of the customer-facing marketing line — "Customers hear only the agent. Agents hear only the customer." — and provides the technical defensibility for that claim against competitive teardown._

## 14.1 Capability Summary

| **Capability**                     | **Specification**                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Speaker enrollment                 | 12-second voice sample at first use; stored as 256-dim voiceprint embedding                            |
| Voiceprint storage                 | On-device, encrypted at rest via secure element (ATECC608B / SE050)                                    |
| Outbound TSE                       | Real-time extraction of agent's voice from 8-mic beam-formed audio; output to Zoo Meet                 |
| Inbound TSE                        | Real-time extraction of customer's voice from incoming call + ambient noise; output to agent's headset |
| Recognition accuracy               | ≥ 98.5% target-speaker identification (EER ≤ 1.5% on enrolled speakers)                                |
| Suppression of non-target speakers | ≥ 24 dB at the same SPL as the target speaker                                                          |
| End-to-end latency (each pipeline) | \< 12 ms (mic capture to processed output)                                                             |
| Concurrent pipelines               | Two (outbound + inbound run in parallel on the NPU)                                                    |
| Voiceprint deletion                | Customer-initiated via Zoo Meet admin console; cryptographic erase + audit log                         |

## 14.2 System Architecture

Voice Lock runs as two parallel inference pipelines on the Pro unit's NPU.

**Pipeline 1 (outbound):** processes the 8-mic beam-formed array audio and emits a single mono channel containing only the enrolled agent's voice; this is the signal Zoo Meet transmits to the customer.

**Pipeline 2 (inbound):** processes the customer call audio plus the Pro unit's far-field reference mics; emits a single mono channel containing only the customer's voice, with all floor noise and neighbor agent voices removed before the audio reaches the agent's headset.

Both pipelines are gated by the agent's stored voiceprint. The Halo Acoustic Arc remains in the system to reduce inter-workstation bleed at the physical layer, making the TSE pipeline's job easier and more accurate.

## 14.3 Headset Integration Modes (USB-C, Dual-Path Voice Lock)

**Voice Lock supports three integration modes selected via firmware on the same USB-C port.**

All three are Year-1 production capabilities; Mode C (ZenaHeadset Quiet) is the deepest integration tier and the primary path for new floor builds, while Modes A and B preserve the install-base sales motion for customers with existing Jabra, Poly, Logitech, Yealink, or EPOS headset fleets.

| **Mode**                               | **How It Works**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | **Target Deployment**                                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A — USB Audio Intercept                | Third-party headset connects to Pro via USB-C; Pro registers as the Zoo Meet audio device and processes inbound audio before passing it to the headset. Supports Jabra Engage 50/55/65/75, Plantronics/Poly Voyager 4200/5200/8200, Logitech Zone Wired/Wireless, Yealink WH62/66, EPOS Impact 700/800/1000.                                              | BPO floors with existing headset fleets — install-base preservation                  |
| B — USB-C-to-Bluetooth Bridge          | Pro acts as a USB-C BLE 5.3 audio source; pairs to any Bluetooth headset (BT 5.0+) and routes processed inbound audio over the wireless link.                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Hot-desk, hybrid, and executive deployments where headsets are personal              |
| C — ZenaHeadset Direct **(PREFERRED)** | ZenaHeadset Quiet connects to Pro via USB-C with deep proprietary protocol. Dual-path Voice Lock: Pro is authoritative when paired; headset's onboard ≥4 TOPS NPU takes over for standalone remote/mobile use. _Dual-path architecture is the key differentiator of Mode C._

_When the agent is at their workstation, Pro drives Voice Lock with its larger NPU._

_When the agent is remote (home office, traveling, mobile work), the headset's on-device NPU and synced voiceprint enable Voice Lock without any other ZenaQuiet hardware._

_The voiceprint never traverses any cloud service — it transfers locally between secure elements via the USB-C wired pairing handshake._

## 14A. Voice Lock Roster — Multi-Voiceprint Floor-Level Cancellation

Voice Lock Roster extends single-speaker Voice Lock (Section 14) to multi-voiceprint subtractive cancellation. The system maintains a roster of up to 64 enrolled voiceprints per floor zone. This capability is unique to ZenaQuiet: no competitor currently maintains a floor-level voiceprint roster integrated with bidirectional TSE.

## 14A.1 Capability Summary

| **Capability**                            | **Specification**                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Roster capacity per floor zone            | Up to 64 enrolled voiceprints (1 user + up to 63 surrounding voices)                                            |
| Roster synchronization protocol           | Beacon-coordinated; voiceprints sync across all Pro units in the same floor zone                                |
| Roster scope boundary                     | Per floor zone (1–4 Beacons). Voiceprints DO NOT sync across floors or buildings without explicit re-consent.   |
| Multi-voiceprint inference                | Parallel subtractive cancellation: target preserved, each enrolled non-target voiceprint individually cancelled |
| Inference latency (16 voiceprints)        | \< 14 ms end-to-end                                                                                             |
| Inference latency (64 voiceprints)        | \< 18 ms end-to-end                                                                                             |
| Suppression of enrolled non-target voices | ≥ 32 dB at matched SPL (vs. ≥24 dB for single-voiceprint baseline Voice Lock)                                   |
| Suppression of unenrolled voices          | Falls back to generic Voice Lock TSE; ≥ 24 dB                                                                   |
| Voiceprint storage                        | On each Pro unit's secure element; encrypted at rest; synced via end-to-end encrypted Beacon mesh               |
| Roster admin                              | Zoo Meet floor admin console — assign agents to floor zones, view roster status, audit logs                     |

## 14A.2 How It Works

1. **Stage 1 — Individual enrollment:** Each agent enrolls their own voiceprint (12-sec sample) at Zoo Meet onboarding. Stored on their own Pro unit's secure element — identical to baseline Voice Lock.
2. **Stage 2 — Floor-zone roster assembly:** When an agent joins a floor zone, Beacon coordinates the propagation of their voiceprint to all other Pro units in that zone. Roster size on each Pro: 1 self + up to 63 peers.
3. **Stage 3 — Multi-voiceprint inference:** On each audio frame, the Pro NPU runs parallel TSE inference: preserve the target voiceprint, individually identify and cancel each enrolled non-target voiceprint, suppress residual unenrolled noise via baseline TSE.

When an agent leaves the floor zone, Beacon issues a cryptographic erase command to all Pro units in that zone, removing their voiceprint and logging the deletion.

**Engineering requirements derived from BIPA (Illinois), GDPR Article 9 (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), CUBI (Texas), WA HB 1493 (Washington), and PIPEDA (Canada):**

- **REQ-VLR-1:** Per-floor-zone enrollment consent is a separate consent layer beyond individual Voice Lock enrollment. Agents must explicitly authorize their voiceprint being stored on peer devices in their floor zone.
- **REQ-VLR-2:** Floor-zone scope is hard-bounded: voiceprints sync only within the explicitly-enumerated Pro units of the authorized zone. Cross-floor and cross-building propagation is prohibited.
- **REQ-VLR-3:** Cross-jurisdiction enrollments require per-jurisdiction consent. An Illinois-resident agent's voiceprint cannot be stored on a Texas-located Pro without BIPA-compliant Illinois consent AND the Texas customer's CUBI compliance.
- **REQ-VLR-4:** Cleaning staff, supervisors, visitors, and other non-enrolled people on the floor are **NEVER** enrolled in the Roster. Their voices are handled by baseline Voice Lock generic suppression only.
- **REQ-VLR-5:** Cryptographic erasure of a departing agent's voiceprint must complete across all peer Pro units within 60 seconds of termination workflow trigger, with audit log on every device.
- **REQ-VLR-6:** Roster admin console workflow must surface BIPA-compliant consent UI to each enrolled agent **BEFORE** their voiceprint is propagated to peer devices. Implicit consent is not sufficient.
- **REQ-VLR-7:** Annual third-party biometric privacy audit specifically scoped to Roster mechanics.
- **REQ-VLR-8:** Agent right-to-erasure must produce a verifiable certificate of deletion from every device that ever held their voiceprint (per GDPR Article 17 and CCPA equivalent).

## Current Designs

### ZenaQuiet Pro

A twin-panel desk-mounted acoustic suppression system designed to sit at a call center agent's workstation. It uses directional speaker arrays for outbound sound masking, a beamforming 8-mic array to capture only the agent's voice, active noise cancellation aimed at the agent's ear, and Zena AI Voice Lock. The goal is that the customer on the line hears only the agent, and the agent hears only the call.

### ZenaQuiet Halo

A lightweight passive 15g acoustic arc that clips onto any standard boom microphone (6–10mm diameter). It has no electronics, no battery, and no DSP — purely passive acoustic redirection and absorption. It's designed to reduce outward speech bleed at the source by 8–12 dB across the speech band.

### ZenaQuiet Beacon

A ceiling-mounted PoE coordinator that manages a cluster of up to 16 Pro workstations on a floor. It handles room acoustic sensing, DSP synchronization across paired Pro units, masking-wash coordination, occupancy detection, OTA firmware updates, and connects to the Zoo Meet admin console for centralized management. It communicates via Wi-Fi dual-band and BLE 5.2.

### ZenaHeadset Quiet

A fully integrated enterprise wireless headset with a **magnetic detachable acoustic arc.** It runs the Zena AI Voice Lock on an onboard NPU (Qualcomm QCS4290), has a 4-mic beamforming boom array plus 2 additional mics inside the arc (6 total), hybrid ANC, Bluetooth 5.3, and a 24-hour battery.
