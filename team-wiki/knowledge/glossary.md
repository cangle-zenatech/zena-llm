# Glossary

Technical terms explained in plain words, A–Z.

## ANC

Active noise cancelling. Microphones pick up outside noise and the speaker plays the opposite sound wave to cancel it. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Research/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Research report 2026-10-26]].

## BOM

Bill of materials. The full list of parts in a product, usually with the cost of each; an "engineering BOM" is the early version kept by the design team. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Pro2 report 2026-10-26]].

## BSP

Board support package. The low-level software that lets an operating system run on one specific circuit board. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Headset/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Headset report 2026-10-26]].

## dB

Decibel. A unit for how loud a sound is. Every 10 dB lower sounds about half as loud. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Directional speaker

A speaker that aims sound at one spot, like a flashlight, so only people in that spot hear it clearly. Also called a "sound bubble" or parametric speaker. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## DVT

Design validation test. The stage after EVT that checks the final design is ready to be made. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## EER

Equal error rate. The point where the system wrongly accepts the wrong person as often as it wrongly rejects the right one. Lower is better. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Epic

A large piece of work in Jira, split into smaller tickets (stories). First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## EVT

Engineering validation test. The stage after PoC that checks early working units meet the specification. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Far-end speaker

The person at the other end of the call (here, the customer). First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Kensington slot

A small standard slot on a device for an anti-theft cable lock, like the one on laptops. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Beacon report 2026-10-26]].

## KN95

A filter standard for face masks: the filter must catch at least 95% of tiny airborne particles. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Mask/overview|ZenaQuiet-Mask]] (MK-6).

## Latency

The delay between sound going in and coming out. Measured in ms (milliseconds, thousandths of a second). First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Listening zone

The small area where the agent can hear the directional speaker. Outside it, speech should not be understandable. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Masking sound

A soft background sound, like a gentle rush of air, played so that nearby people can't make out speech. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## MCUboot

A small secure start-up program that checks a firmware update is genuine before running it, and can swap back to the old version if the update fails. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Beacon report 2026-10-26]].

## NPU

Neural processing unit. A part of a chip built to run AI models fast and with little power. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Research/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Research report 2026-10-26]].

## OTA

Over the air. Sending a software update to a device through the network instead of plugging it in. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Beacon report 2026-10-26]].

## PoC

Proof of concept. An early test to show an idea can work before building the real product. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## PoE

Power over Ethernet. The network cable also carries the power, so the device needs no separate power plug. PoE+ carries more power. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Beacon report 2026-10-26]].

## Quantization

Storing an AI model's numbers with fewer digits (for example 8 or 16 bits) so it runs faster on small chips, sometimes at a cost in sound quality. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Research/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Research report 2026-10-26]].

## Revision A / Revision B

Successive versions of a prototype within one stage. Revision A is the first EVT-1 build of the Pro; Revision B fixes what Revision A showed. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Pro2 report 2026-10-26]].

## Sprint

A fixed work period in Jira (here two weeks). Tickets planned for a sprint should be finished by its end. First seen: [[ZenaQuiet-Headset/jira/2026-10-26-jira|Headset report 2026-10-26]].

## TPS

Not spelled out in the source. This likely means a technical product specification: the document listing what the finished product must achieve. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## TSE

Target Speaker Extraction. Software that picks out one person's voice and removes background noise and other voices. The team uses an open-source model called Mask2Flow-TSE. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].
