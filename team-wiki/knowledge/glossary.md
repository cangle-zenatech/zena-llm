# Glossary

Technical terms explained in plain words, A–Z.

## dB
Decibel. A unit for how loud a sound is. Every 10 dB lower sounds about half as loud. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Directional speaker
A speaker that aims sound at one spot, like a flashlight, so only people in that spot hear it clearly. Also called a "sound bubble" or parametric speaker. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Epic
A large piece of work in Jira, split into smaller tickets (stories). First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Far-end speaker
The person at the other end of the call (here, the customer). First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Latency
The delay between sound going in and coming out. Measured in ms (milliseconds, thousandths of a second). First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## Listening zone
The small area where the agent can hear the directional speaker. Outside it, speech should not be understandable. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## PoC
Proof of concept. An early test to show an idea can work before building the real product. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## TPS
Not spelled out in the source. This likely means a technical product specification: the document listing what the finished product must achieve. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].

## TSE
Target Speaker Extraction. Software that picks out one person's voice and removes background noise and other voices. The team uses an open-source model called Mask2Flow-TSE. First seen: [[2026-06-18-PRO2-2|PRO2-2]].
