## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** The Beacon is a ceiling-mounted unit powered over the network cable ([[glossary#PoE|PoE]], Power over Ethernet). It coordinates a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, passes cloud commands on to them over MQTT (a messaging protocol) and coordinates zone settings (BC-107, BC-164). It also has a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound-masking speaker. It senses room noise and tells the Pros how to adjust their audio, while the Pro does the audio processing itself (BC-50, BC-153, BC-191, BC-233, BC-234).
