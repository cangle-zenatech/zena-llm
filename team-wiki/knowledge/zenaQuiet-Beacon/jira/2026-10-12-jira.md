## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Beacon

**Purpose:** A ceiling-mounted, PoE-powered coordinator for a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, relays cloud commands over MQTT and coordinates zone settings (BC-106, BC-107, BC-164). It also carries a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound masking module (BC-50, BC-191).

**Technical requirements by Epic:** (Epic descriptions re-read 2026-10-12; no change in content seen versus the 2026-10-10 summary.)

  - No technical specs in the Epic description
- **BC-38** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-09-06, 7/12 Stories done, variance −311d)
