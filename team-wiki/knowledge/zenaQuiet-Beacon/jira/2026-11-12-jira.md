## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** The Beacon is a ceiling-mounted unit powered over the network cable ([[glossary#PoE|PoE]], Power over Ethernet). It coordinates a floor cluster of up to 16 ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors them, enforces voiceprint consent and passes cloud commands on to them over MQTT (a messaging protocol) (BC-107, BC-164). It also has a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and now the sound-masking function. It senses room noise and tells the Pros how to adjust their audio (BC-50, BC-153, BC-191, BC-233, BC-234).
