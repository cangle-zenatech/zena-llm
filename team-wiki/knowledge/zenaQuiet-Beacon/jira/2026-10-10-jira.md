## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Beacon

**Purpose:** A ceiling-mounted, PoE-powered coordinator for a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, relays cloud commands over MQTT and coordinates zone settings (BC-106, BC-107, BC-164). It also carries a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound masking module (BC-50, BC-191).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- **BC-23** Product Industrial Design (due 2027-09-06, 80% Stories done, variance −405d)
  - 6-step ID process; now at 5/6 Design Refinement (BC-153) [ID]
  - No technical specs in the Epic description
- **BC-38** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-09-06, 58% done, variance −311d)
  - STM32H743 MCU, SDRAM and QSPI Flash, ATECC608B secure element [EE]
  - PoE power, Ethernet PHY and RJ45, USB-C service port, MEMS mic, RGB LED, reset button [EE]
  - EVT-1: power management, Ethernet and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, memory, sound masking module (BC-187 to BC-191) [EE]
- **BC-50** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-09-06, 40% done, variance −219d)
  - Enclosure 150 mm diameter × 36 mm; passive cooling, no fan [ME]
  - Ceiling mount (drop tile and drywall) passes a 5× weight (1.6 kg) pull-out test [ME]
  - 360° LED ring visible at 1 m; ceiling mic within ±2 dB of open air through an IP4X mesh [ME, AC]
  - Passive thermal at 25.5 W PoE+; dual-band RF clearance; CE/FCC readiness [ME, EE]
- **BC-106** Connectivity between Cloud & Beacon (due 2027-09-06, 100% Stories done, variance −378d)
  - MQTT to an EMQX broker, data stored in PostgreSQL; telemetry from up to 12 Pros [FW]
  - EVT goal: push a software update once to every device on the floor [FW]
- **BC-107** Connectivity between Pro & Beacon (due 2027-09-06, 56% done, variance −370d)
  - Register and monitor up to 12 Pros per Beacon; TLS/mTLS; detect duplicate registrations [FW, SW]
  - EVT-1: multi-device management, command relay, session connections, room-noise sensing (BC-113 to BC-117) [FW, SW]
- **BC-164** Beacon firmware development (due 2027-09-06, 74% done, variance −304d)
  - Zephyr RTOS on STM32H743 (NUCLEO-H743ZI and custom board) [FW]
  - OLIC-Z over mutual TLS 1.3, mDNS discovery, MQTT 3.1.1 over TLS, MCUboot signed boot [FW]
  - Consent management and crypto-erase; open: production signing key, OTA, hardening [FW]
- **BC-165** Field Test+RC (2027-02-08 → 2027-09-06, 0% done)
