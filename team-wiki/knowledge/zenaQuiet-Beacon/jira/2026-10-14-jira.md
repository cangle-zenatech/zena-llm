## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A ceiling-mounted, PoE-powered coordinator for a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, relays cloud commands over MQTT and coordinates zone settings (BC-107, BC-164). It also carries a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound masking module, and senses room noise to tell Pros how to adjust audio (BC-50, BC-191, BC-233).

**Technical requirements by Epic:** (Epic descriptions re-read 2026-10-14.)

- **BC-23** Product Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 4/5 Stories done, variance −280d)
  - 6-step ID process; now at 5/6 Design Refinement (BC-153, still To Do) [ID]
  - No technical specs in the Epic description
- **BC-38** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 7/12 Stories done, variance −280d)
  - STM32H743 MCU, SDRAM and QSPI Flash, ATECC608B secure element [EE]
  - PoE power, Ethernet PHY and RJ45, USB-C service port, MEMS mic, RGB LED, reset button [EE]

- **BC-50** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-11-15, 6/15 Stories done, variance −280d)

  - 360° LED ring visible at 1 m; ceiling mic within ±2 dB of open air through an IP4X mesh [ME, AC]
