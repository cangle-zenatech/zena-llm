## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A ceiling-mounted, PoE-powered (power over the network cable) coordinator for a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, relays cloud commands over MQTT and coordinates zone settings (BC-107, BC-164). It also carries a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound masking speaker, and senses room noise to tell the Pros how to adjust their audio; the Beacon only senses and directs, the Pro does the audio processing (BC-50, BC-153, BC-191, BC-233, BC-234).
