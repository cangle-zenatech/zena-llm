## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** The Beacon is a ceiling-mounted unit powered over the network cable (PoE). It coordinates a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units: it registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, relays cloud commands over MQTT (a messaging protocol) and coordinates zone settings (BC-107, BC-164). It also carries a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound-masking speaker. It senses room noise and tells the Pros how to adjust their audio; the Beacon only senses and directs, and the Pro does the audio processing (BC-50, BC-153, BC-191, BC-233, BC-234).
