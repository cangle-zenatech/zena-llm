## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A ceiling-mounted, PoE-powered coordinator for a floor cluster of ZenaQuiet Pro units. It registers and monitors the Pros, enforces voiceprint consent, relays cloud commands over MQTT and coordinates zone settings (BC-107, BC-164). It also carries a room microphone, a 360° LED ring and a sound masking module, and senses room noise to tell Pros how to adjust audio (BC-50, BC-191, BC-233, BC-234).

**Technical requirements by Epic:** (Epic descriptions re-read 2026-10-15.)

- **BC-23** Product Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 4/5 Stories done, variance −280d)
  - 6-step ID process; now at 5/6 Design Refinement (BC-153, still To Do; adds a sound-masking speaker and LED status handling) [ID]
  - No technical specs in the Epic description
- **BC-38** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 7/12 Stories done, variance −280d)
  - STM32H743 MCU, SDRAM and QSPI Flash, ATECC608B secure element [EE]
  - PoE power, Ethernet PHY and RJ45, USB-C service port, MEMS mic, RGB LED, reset button [EE]
  - EVT-1: power, Ethernet and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, memory, remaining functions, sound masking (BC-187 to BC-191), each split into component selection → schematic → PCB layout (BC-218 to BC-232) [EE]
- **BC-50** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-11-15, 6/15 Stories done, variance −280d)
  - Enclosure 150 mm diameter × 36 mm; passive cooling, no fan; DSP coordination across "up to 16 Pro units" [ME]
  - Ceiling mount (drop tile and drywall) passes a 5× weight (1.6 kg) pull-out test [ME]
  - 360° LED ring visible at 1 m; ceiling mic within ±2 dB of open air through an IP4X mesh [ME, AC]
