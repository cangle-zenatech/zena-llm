## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Headset

**Purpose:** A call-centre (BPO) headset, "ZenaHeadset Quiet", for agents who wear it 8–12 hours a shift. It captures the agent's voice cleanly for the Voice Lock speech pipeline (HS-1), cancels room noise at the agent's ears with hybrid ANC (HS-2), and uses a clip-on magnetic Arc to reduce how much neighbours overhear the agent (HS-51). It connects over USB-C and Bluetooth and integrates with Zoo Meet and the ZenaAI cloud (HS-380, HS-451).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- **HS-1** Acoustic Layer 3, Inbound Audio Capture at Headset's Microphone (due 2027-09-06, 0/13 Stories done, variance −308d; no owner)
  - 4 × MEMS digital mic boom array with beam-forming; EER ≤1.5% into Voice Lock TSE; STI ≥0.75 at Zoo Meet input [EE, FW, AC (inferred)]
  - Phase 1: basic single boom mic capture, VAD and noise suppression firmware, SoC selection with audio DSP SDK (HS-144, HS-161, HS-164) [ME, EE, FW]
  - Phase 2: single-mic SNR ≥25 dB at 300–700 mm; VAD false positive ≤2%, false negative ≤1% [FW]
  - Description asks for DSP help from the mic supplier (Spider Vision Sensors); no response recorded [EE, FW]
- **HS-2** Acoustic Layer 2, Outbound Audio with Active Noise Cancelling (due 2027-09-06, 0/10 Stories done, variance −334d; no owner)
  - Hybrid ANC (feedforward + feedback mics), ≥25 dB broadband cancellation 20 Hz–1 kHz; ANC path latency ≤1.5 ms, no oscillation [EE, FW, AC (inferred)]
  - Phase 1: earcup acoustic seal and cushion spec; passive baseline across 5th–95th percentile heads incl. glasses (HS-13) [ME]
  - Description states the team has no documented ANC experience; EVT-2 targets need an ANC specialist or consultant [AC, FW]
- **HS-3** Power Supply & Battery Management & Fast Charging (due 2027-09-06, 2/12 Stories done, variance n/a; Phong.ngoc.nguyen)
  - ≥8 h wireless use at full DSP + ANC + audio load; 1,200 mAh Li-Po; ≥500 cycles at ≥80% capacity [EE]
  - USB-C PD fast charge: 0–50% in ≤20 min, 0–100% in ≤60 min, without hurting cycle life (HS-418, HS-422) [EE, FW, SW]
  - BMS IC, power rails, battery safety certification, production-line battery EOL test (HS-102, HS-103) [EE, FA (inferred)]
- **HS-4** User Interface (Control & Status) (due 2027-09-06, 0/5 Stories done, variance −306d; no owner)
  - Physical buttons for mute, ANC, volume and Zena AI assistant; power/pairing slider; 360° Voice Lock LED (HS-117, HS-118) [ME, EE, FW, SW]
  - All controls respond within 200 ms; operable by touch alone; work after IP54 sweat test [FW, ME]
- **HS-6** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-09-06, 3/22 Stories done, variance −308d; Nguyen Trung Tin)
  - IP54; 1.5 m drop without Arc detaching; 10,000 Arc attach/detach cycles; ≤180 g without Arc [ME]
  - Battery isolation and puncture protection next to a live NPU (HS-350) [ME, EE]
  - Earcup shell, 90° fold, headband fit on 52/57/62 cm head forms, Nitinol headband (HS-124, HS-580, HS-587, HS-354) [ME]
- **HS-47** "stop creating trash and assign to me" (due 2027-09-06, no Stories)
