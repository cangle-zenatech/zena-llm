## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A call-centre (BPO) headset, "ZenaHeadset Quiet", for agents who wear it 8–12 hours a shift. It captures the agent's voice cleanly for the Voice Lock speech pipeline (HS-1), cancels room noise at the agent's ears with hybrid noise cancelling (HS-2), and uses a clip-on magnetic Arc to reduce how much neighbours overhear the agent (HS-51). It connects over USB-C and Bluetooth and works with Zoo Meet and the ZenaAI cloud (HS-380, HS-451). Epic titles, owners and dates are unchanged since 2026-10-13 (`diff`).
