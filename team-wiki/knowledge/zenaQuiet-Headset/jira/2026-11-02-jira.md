## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A headset for call-centre (BPO) agents who wear it for 8–12 hours a shift. It captures the agent's voice cleanly for the Voice Lock speech pipeline (HS-1) and cancels room noise at the ears (HS-2). A clip-on magnetic Arc is meant to reduce how much neighbours overhear (HS-51). It connects over USB-C and Bluetooth to Zoo Meet and the ZenaAI cloud (HS-380, HS-451). No Epic title, owner, date or description changed since 2026-10-26 (`diff` and a description comparison).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- HS-1 ACOUSTIC LAYER 3_Inbound Audio Capture at Headset's Microphone (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance 0d, To Do)
  - Boom mic array of 4 MEMS (tiny chip) digital mics with beam-forming, which aims the mics at the agent's mouth. Voice goes to the Voice Lock TSE model (target speaker extraction: it keeps only the enrolled agent's voice) with EER (equal error rate) ≤1.5% [EE, FW, AC (inferred)]
  - POC: single boom mic capture, voice detection and noise suppression firmware, chip selection (HS-144, HS-161, HS-164), none started [ME, EE, FW]. Jira plans the NXP i.MX 95 chip (HS-154, HS-155, HS-162, HS-165, HS-166) [EE, FW]
  - Later phases: single-mic SNR (signal-to-noise ratio) ≥25 dB at 300–700 mm; voice detection false positive ≤2%, false negative ≤1% [FW]
