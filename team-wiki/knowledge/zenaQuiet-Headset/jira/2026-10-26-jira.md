## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A call-centre (BPO) headset for agents who wear it 8–12 hours a shift. It captures the agent's voice cleanly for the Voice Lock speech pipeline (HS-1) and cancels room noise at the ears (HS-2). A clip-on magnetic Arc reduces how much neighbours overhear (HS-51). It connects over USB-C and Bluetooth to Zoo Meet and the ZenaAI cloud (HS-380, HS-451). Epic titles, owners and dates are unchanged since 2026-10-19 (`diff`).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- HS-1 ACOUSTIC LAYER 3_Inbound Audio Capture at Headset's Microphone (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance 0d, To Do)
  - Boom mic array of 4 MEMS (tiny chip) digital mics with beam-forming, which aims the mics at the agent's mouth; voice goes to the Voice Lock TSE model (target speaker extraction, which keeps only the enrolled agent's voice) with EER (equal error rate) ≤1.5% [EE, FW, AC (inferred)]
  - POC: single boom mic capture, voice detection and noise suppression firmware, chip selection (HS-144, HS-161, HS-164) [ME, EE, FW]. Jira plans the NXP i.MX 95 chip (HS-162, HS-165, HS-166); HS-581 "System on Module searching" is Done [EE, FW]
