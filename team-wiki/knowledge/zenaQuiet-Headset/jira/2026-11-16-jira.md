## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** The ZenaQuiet Headset is for call-centre (BPO) agents who wear it for 8–12 hours a shift. It picks up the agent's voice cleanly for the Voice Lock speech pipeline (HS-1) and cancels room noise at the ears with ANC, active noise cancelling (HS-2). A clip-on magnetic Arc with two extra microphones is meant to limit how much neighbours overhear (HS-51). The headset connects over USB-C and Bluetooth (HS-451). It is sold as a bundle with the Pro and Beacon (`transcripts/2026-11-16-standup.md · Cang Le 1:04:57`).
