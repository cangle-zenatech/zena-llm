## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Pro2

**Purpose:** A desk device for call-centre agents that gives private calls without a headset. An 8-mic array plus target speaker extraction (TSE) captures only the agent's voice for the customer. A directional ultrasonic speaker delivers the cleaned customer audio only to the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- **PRO2-1** Layer 3 — Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (agent voice capture) (due 2027-09-06, 50% Stories done, variance −280d)
  - Capture target speech with an 8-mic array; suppress noise and competing speech [FW, EE]
  - MVDR beamforming to a 30°–150° sector, then TSE; combined suppression spec 15–20 dB [FW, AC]
  - EVT-1: mic port geometry/MEMS alignment, PCB vibration isolation, acoustic baffling [ME, AC]
- **PRO2-2** Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-09-06, 43% done, variance −314d)
  - Clean incoming customer audio with Mask2Flow-TSE [FW, AC]
  - Directional speaker: private audio to the agent, 120° front, adjustable angle [EE, ME]
  - EVT-1: acoustic side baffle; joint test plan and IEC 62471 protocol (PRO2-434) [ME, EE, FW]
- **PRO2-3** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-09-06, 31% done, variance −12d)
  - 24 V / 72 W DC adapter; USB-C, USB-A, Gigabit Ethernet; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth [EE]
  - New EVT-1 hardware Stories: power, USB, Ethernet/wireless, memory, AI accelerator, beamforming mic and speaker, touch, LED, cooling fan (PRO2-457 to PRO2-466) [EE]

- **PRO2-8** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-09-06, 8% done, variance −249d)
