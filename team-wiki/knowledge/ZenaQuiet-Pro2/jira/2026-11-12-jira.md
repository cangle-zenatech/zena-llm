## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** ZenaQuiet-Pro2 is a desk device for call-centre agents that keeps calls private without a headset. A microphone array and a voice-isolation model send only the agent's voice to the customer; the model is called TSE, "target speaker extraction". A directional ultrasonic speaker plays the cleaned customer audio only inside the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449). Sound masking, background noise that covers nearby voices, is no longer part of the Pro; it moved to the Beacon (PRO2-156, 2026-11-09).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- PRO2-1 Layer 3  — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE(agent voice capture) (due 2027-11-15, 45% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - Capture the agent's speech with an 8-mic array and suppress background noise and other speakers [EE, FW]
  - Beamforming (aiming the mics electronically) to a 30°–150° sector, then the TSE model keyed to the agent's enrolled voiceprint [FW, AC]
  - Capture targets: mic port loss ≤1 dB, structure-borne noise <20 dB SPL(A) (PRO2-22, PRO2-26); silicone O-ring standoff, Shore A 30–40 (PRO2-23 Done); mic-array baffling against reflected ultrasonic sound still to be tested (PRO2-150) [ME, AC]
  - EVT-1 firmware: better TSE (PRO2-526), power management (PRO2-527), secure voiceprint with no audio leaving the device (PRO2-528) [FW]
- PRO2-2 Layer 1  —  Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-11-15, 57% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - Clean the incoming customer audio with TSE, then play it through a directional ultrasonic speaker aimed at the agent [FW, AC]
  - Private audio inside the listening zone, little intelligible leakage outside it [EE, ME, AC]
  - Speaker side baffle closed after sound masking moved to the Beacon (PRO2-156 Done); outbound TSE needs the new board (PRO2-397) [ME, AC, FW]
- PRO2-3 Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 29% done, variance -70d, In Progress)

- PRO2-4 User Interface (Control and Status) (due 2027-11-15, 25% done, variance -280d, To Do)
