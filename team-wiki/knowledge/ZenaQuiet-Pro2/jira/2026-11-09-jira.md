## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** ZenaQuiet-Pro2 is a desk device for call-centre agents that keeps calls private without a headset. A microphone array and a voice-isolation model send only the agent's voice to the customer; the model is called TSE, "target speaker extraction". A directional ultrasonic speaker plays the cleaned customer audio only inside the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- PRO2-1 Layer 3  — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE(agent voice capture) (due 2027-11-15, 45% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - Capture the agent's speech with an 8-mic array; suppress background noise and other speakers [EE, FW]
  - Beamforming to a 30°–150° sector, then the TSE model keyed to the agent's enrolled voiceprint [FW, AC]
  - Capture targets: mic port loss ≤1 dB, structure-borne noise <20 dB SPL(A), masking wash >30 dB below the voice (PRO2-22, PRO2-150); first standoff choice is a silicone O-ring, Shore A 30–40 (PRO2-23 Done) [ME, AC]
  - EVT-1 firmware: better TSE (PRO2-526), power management (PRO2-527; power-state study PRO2-530 Done), secure voiceprint with no audio leaving the device (PRO2-528) [FW]
- PRO2-2 Layer 1  —  Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-11-15, 43% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - Clean the incoming customer audio with TSE, then play it through a directional ultrasonic speaker aimed at the agent [FW, AC]
  - Private audio inside the listening zone, little intelligible leakage outside it [EE, ME, AC]
  - EVT: acoustic side baffle (PRO2-156); joint deliverables and test support (PRO2-434, PRO2-435) [ME, AC]

- **PRO2-8 Mechanical structure**
  - Being discussed: how much cooling the compact housing needs, and how loud it is.
  - Thinking so far: a bench mock-up with a 12 W heat source was tested. The 12 W is the worst case for the main board: 5 W i.MX95 main chip, 3 W HAILO AI accelerator and 4 W for the rest. With the cover on, the heat sink settled at 65°C with two fans and 60°C with three. Without fans it reached 100°C. All three fans together measured 45 dB(A) (PRO2-344, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-03).
  - Decided: use at least one side inlet fan plus one outlet fan on top of the heat sink. Fan speed runs at 30% below 40°C and rises to 100% at 80°C. Keep the i.MX95 below 65°C at full load, 20% under an assumed 85°C limit (PRO2-344, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-03). This relaxes the earlier simulation advice of three same-size fans (PRO2-344, Duy Nguyen, 2026-10-19). PRO2-344 and the vent check PRO2-367 were closed on 2026-11-03.
