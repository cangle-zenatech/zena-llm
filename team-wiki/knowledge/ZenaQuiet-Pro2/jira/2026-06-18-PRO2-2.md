# [PRO2-2] Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (sound bubble)

> **TL;DR:** This [[glossary#Epic|epic]] (a large piece of work split into smaller tickets) cleans up the caller's voice and beams it only to the agent, so calls stay private without a headset.

## What this is

This epic is part of [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/overview|ZenaQuiet-Pro2]]. The team is building a [[glossary#PoC|PoC]] (proof of concept: an early test to show an idea can work). First, an open-source [[glossary#TSE|TSE]] (Target Speaker Extraction: software that picks out one voice and removes background noise) model called Mask2Flow-TSE cleans the customer's audio. Then a [[glossary#Directional speaker|directional speaker]] (a speaker that aims sound at one spot, like a flashlight) plays it to the agent. People outside the agent's [[glossary#Listening zone|listening zone]] (the small area where the sound can be heard) should not understand the speech.

A separate piece of work, Layer 2, handles noise around the agent. Removing noise on the customer's side is not part of this epic.

## Why it matters

Agents could take private calls without wearing a headset. The finished product must hit six targets set in the [[glossary#TPS|TPS]] (section 14.7). Examples:

- Recognise the right speaker at least 98.5% of the time.
- Cut background noise by at least 35 [[glossary#dB|dB]] (decibels; it sounds roughly ten times quieter).
- Add under 12 ms of [[glossary#Latency|latency]] (delay: about one hundredth of a second, too short to notice).

The reporter, [[reference#People|Hung Pham Nguyen]], warns these targets may need rewriting by engineers. He also asks how this works with Zoo Meet: cleaning audio in the cloud is expensive and is usually built into hardware.

## Architecture flow

```text
   +-------------------+
   | Far-end speaker   |   (the customer)
   +-------------------+
             |
             v
   +-------------------+
   | Zoo Meet /        |
   | ZenaComm          |   (call software)
   +-------------------+
             |
             v
   +-------------------+
   | TSE / audio       |   removes background noise
   | processing        |
   +-------------------+
             |
             v   clean customer speech
   +-------------------+
   | Directional       |   aims the sound
   | speaker           |
   +-------------------+
             |
             v
   +-------------------+
   | Listening zone    |   little understandable sound leaks outside
   +-------------------+
```

The customer's voice arrives through the call software, gets cleaned, and is beamed only to the agent's spot.
