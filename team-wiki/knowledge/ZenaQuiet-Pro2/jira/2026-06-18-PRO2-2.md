# PRO2-2 — Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE (sound bubble)

## In plain words

Call-centre agents normally wear a headset to hear customers. This Epic (a large piece of work split
into smaller tickets) aims to remove the headset. The customer's voice is first cleaned of background
noise by software, then played through a **directional speaker** that aims sound at the agent, so only
the agent can understand it. The early test stage (POC) is finished; work is now in the engineering
test stage (EVT), with the Epic due 2027-09-06.

## How the sound travels

1. The customer speaks on the call (through Zoo Meet or ZenaComm, the calling software).
2. **TSE** software (picks out one voice and removes background noise) cleans the customer's audio.
   The team uses an open-source model called Mask2Flow-TSE.
3. A **directional speaker** aims the clean audio at the agent's **listening zone**.
4. Outside that zone, speech should not be understandable.

The ticket says plainly: noise on the customer's side is cleaned here, but separating the customer's
own voice from other voices in their room is **not** part of this Epic.

## What the ticket asks for

**To finish the POC stage** (all of these at once):

- Clean customer audio is clear to someone at the agent's ear inside the zone.
- Outside the zone, it can't be understood.
- Delay stays within budget.
- Audio really arrives from Zoo Meet / ZenaComm through a working connection, not just on paper.

**Targets for the finished product** (from the TPS, the product specification, §14.7). The ticket
notes these are much higher than the current POC results.

| Target                                       | Number               | In plain words                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recognises the right agent's voice           | ≥ 98.5% (EER ≤ 1.5%) | Gets it right at least 98.5 times in 100                               |
| Turns down other people's voices             | ≥ 24 dB              | Other voices sound roughly a fifth as loud                             |
| Turns down background noise                  | ≥ 35 dB, 100 Hz–8 kHz | Background noise sounds roughly a tenth as loud, across normal speech pitches |
| Delay through the voice software             | < 12 ms              | Under 12 thousandths of a second: not noticeable                       |
| Wrongly accepts a different agent            | ≤ 0.5%               | At most 1 in 200 tries                                                 |
| Wrongly rejects the right agent (cold start) | ≤ 2.0%               | At most 1 in 50 tries                                                  |

"Roughly a fifth / a tenth as loud" uses the glossary rule of thumb (every 10 dB lower sounds about
half as loud) **(inferred)**.

## Open questions and risks

- **Where does the noise cleaning run?** The ticket asks how TSE works with Zoo Meet, since cleaning
  audio in the cloud is costly and is usually built into the device.
- **Targets may change.** The ticket says the two noise-reduction targets, and possibly all targets,
  may need rewriting from an engineering point of view.
- **⚠ Unclear EVT goal.** The EVT section describes playing a soft masking sound outward so neighbours
  hear a wash instead of speech. A note under it says "this should be Directional Sound feature".
  It's not clear whether masking belongs in this Epic or in Layer 2.
- **EVT work not dated.** PRO2-434 and PRO2-435 have no due date; PRO2-156 is due 2026-10-21 and
  has not started.
