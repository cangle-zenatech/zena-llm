## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A desk device for call-centre agents that gives private calls without a headset. A microphone array plus a voice-isolation model (TSE, "target speaker extraction") sends only the agent's voice to the customer, and a directional ultrasonic speaker plays the cleaned customer audio only inside the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- PRO2-1 Layer 3  — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE(agent voice capture) (due 2027-11-15, 45% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - Capture the agent's speech with an 8-mic array; suppress background noise and other speakers [EE, FW]
  - Beamforming to a 30°–150° sector, then the TSE model keyed to the agent's enrolled voiceprint [FW, AC]
  - Capture targets: mic port loss ≤1 dB, structure-borne noise <20 dB SPL(A), masking wash >30 dB below the voice (PRO2-22, PRO2-150) [ME, AC]
  - EVT-1 firmware: better TSE (PRO2-526), power management (PRO2-527), secure voiceprint, boot and OTA with no audio leaving the device (PRO2-528) [FW]
- PRO2-2 Layer 1  —  Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-11-15, 43% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - Clean the incoming customer audio with TSE, then play it through a directional speaker aimed at the agent [FW, AC]
  - Private audio inside the listening zone, little intelligible leakage outside it [EE, ME, AC]
  - EVT: acoustic side baffle (PRO2-156); joint deliverables and test support (PRO2-434, PRO2-435) [ME, AC]
- PRO2-3 Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 29% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - 24 V / 72 W DC adapter with PoE backup; USB-C, USB-A, Gigabit Ethernet; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth [EE]
  - EVT-1 hardware Stories PRO2-457 to PRO2-466 (power, USB, network, memory, AI accelerator, mic and speaker boards, touch, LED, fan); all 63 Subtasks Done, Stories in review [EE]
- PRO2-4 User Interface (Control and Status) (due 2027-11-15, 0% done, variance -280d, To Do)
  - One interface to control core functions and show status [EE, ME]
  - Touch controls through a copper plate behind the panel, ring-light status, anti-theft tether, manual feedback UI (from child titles and comments, inferred) [EE, ME]
- PRO2-8 Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-11-15, 20% done, variance -70d, In Progress)
  - House the i.MX95 board, cooling and speaker panel in a compact unibody with a torque hinge (PRO2-416, PRO2-422) [ME, EE]
  - 76 cm drop on all 6 faces (IEC 60068-2-31); IP30 (IEC 60529); stable on a desk tilted ≥10° [ME]
  - Static analyses for the EVT-1 enclosure (PRO2-541 to PRO2-546) and a 3-fan cooling layout (PRO2-344) [ME]
- PRO2-172 PRO Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 83% done, variance -280d, In Progress)
