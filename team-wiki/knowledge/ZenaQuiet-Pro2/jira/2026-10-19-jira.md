## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A desk device for call-centre agents that gives private calls without a headset. A microphone array plus a voice-isolation model (TSE, "target speaker extraction") sends only the agent's voice to the customer, and a directional ultrasonic speaker plays the cleaned customer audio only inside the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic** (requirement text from the Epic descriptions; Epic titles as in Jira today):

- **PRO2-1** Layer 3 — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE (agent voice capture) (due 2027-11-15, 5/11 Stories done, variance −70d)
  - Capture the agent's speech with an 8-mic array; suppress background noise and other speakers [EE, FW]
  - MVDR beamforming to a 30°–150° sector, then the Mask2flow TSE model keyed to the agent's enrolled voiceprint [FW, AC]
  - Hardware capture targets: mic port loss ≤1 dB, structure-borne noise <20 dB SPL(A), masking wash >30 dB below the voice (PRO2-11 now Done, PRO2-22, PRO2-150) [ME, AC]
  - EVT-1 firmware: better TSE for same-gender overlap and low latency (PRO2-526), lower i.MX95 power and temperature (PRO2-527), protected voiceprint storage, secure boot and secure OTA (PRO2-528) [FW]
- **PRO2-2** Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-11-15, 3/7 done, variance −70d)
  - Clean the incoming customer audio with Mask2Flow-TSE, then play it through a directional speaker aimed at the agent [FW, AC]
  - Private audio inside the listening zone, little intelligible leakage outside it [EE, ME, AC]
  - EVT: acoustic side baffle (PRO2-156, Subtasks dated 2026-11-02 → 2026-11-13); joint deliverables and test support (PRO2-434, PRO2-435) [ME, AC]
- **PRO2-3** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 5/17 done, variance −70d)
