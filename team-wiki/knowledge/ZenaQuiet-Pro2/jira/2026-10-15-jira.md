## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A desk device for call-centre agents that gives private calls without a headset. An 8-microphone array plus a voice-isolation model (TSE, "target speaker extraction") sends only the agent's voice to the customer. A directional speaker plays the cleaned customer audio only inside the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic** (Epic descriptions re-read 2026-10-15; no Epic was edited after 2026-10-13, so the content is the same as the 2026-10-14 report):

- **PRO2-1** Layer 3 — Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (agent voice capture) (due 2027-11-15, 4/8 Stories done, variance −70d)
  - Capture the agent's speech with an 8-mic array; suppress background noise and other speakers [EE, FW]
  - Two layers: MVDR beamforming narrows pickup to a 30°–150° sector, then the Mask2flow TSE model, keyed to the agent's enrolled voiceprint (WaveLM), removes other voices [FW, AC]
  - Hardware capture targets: mic port loss ≤1 dB, structure-borne noise <20 dB SPL(A), masking wash >30 dB below the voice (EVT-1 Stories PRO2-11, PRO2-22, PRO2-150) [ME, AC]
  - Combined suppression must reach 15–20 dB; today ~12 dB. The text gives the TSE model score both as "current best +3.978 dB" and "current status +1.6 dB" against a +2.5 dB target (contradiction, see Data Gaps). EVT section still "TBA"
- **PRO2-2** Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-11-15, 3/7 done, variance −70d)
  - Clean incoming customer audio with Mask2Flow-TSE, then play it through a directional speaker aimed at the agent [FW, AC]
  - Private audio inside the listening zone, little intelligible leakage outside it [EE, ME, AC]
  - EVT: acoustic side baffle (PRO2-156); joint deliverables and test support (PRO2-434, PRO2-435) [ME, AC]
- **PRO2-3** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 5/16 done, variance −70d)
  - 24 V / 72 W DC adapter with PoE backup; USB-C (USB 3.2 + power out), USB-A (5 V + debug), Gigabit Ethernet; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth [EE]
  - EVT-1 hardware Stories: power, USB, Ethernet/wireless, memory, AI accelerator, beamforming mic and speaker, touch, LED, cooling fan (PRO2-457 to PRO2-466) [EE]
- **PRO2-4** User Interface (Control and Status) (due 2027-11-15, 0/4 done, variance −280d)
  - One interface to control core functions and show status with clear feedback [EE, ME]
  - Touch controls, ring-light status, anti-theft tether, manual feedback UI (from child titles, inferred) [EE, ME]
- **PRO2-8** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-11-15, 2/25 done, variance −70d)
  - House the i.MX 95 board, a 1.4 kg steel core and a 6 mm heat pipe in a 244 mm octagonal base with two 180 × 220 × 22 mm panels [ME, EE]
  - Survive a 76 cm drop on all 6 faces (IEC 60068-2-31); IP30 dust protection (IEC 60529); stable on a desk tilted ≥10° [ME]
- **PRO2-172** PRO Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 5/6 done, variance −280d)
