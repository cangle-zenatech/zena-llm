## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Pro2

**Purpose:** A desk device for call-centre agents that gives private calls without a headset. An 8-mic array plus target speaker extraction (TSE) captures only the agent's voice for the customer. A directional speaker plays the cleaned customer audio only inside the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic** (Epic descriptions re-read 2026-10-13; no change in content from the last report):

- **PRO2-1** Layer 3 — Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (agent voice capture) (due 2027-11-15, 4/8 Stories done, variance −70d)
  - Capture target speech with an 8-mic array; suppress noise and competing speech [EE, FW]
  - MVDR beamforming to a 30°–150° sector, then TSE (ECAPA-TDNN voiceprint + Conv-TasNet). Combined suppression spec is 15–20 dB; current is ~12 dB. TSE gain is +1.6 dB vs. a +2.5 dB target [FW, AC]
  - Hardware capture targets: mic port insertion loss ≤1 dB, structure-borne noise <20 dB SPL(A), masking wash >30 dB below the voice (EVT-1 Stories PRO2-11, PRO2-22, PRO2-150) [ME, AC]
  - The EVT section of the Epic says "TBA"; KPIs are flagged for rewrite with the CTO
- **PRO2-2** Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-11-15, 3/7 done, variance −70d)
  - Clean incoming customer audio with Mask2Flow-TSE: ≥24 dB non-target suppression, ≥35 dB noise suppression, <12 ms latency [FW, AC]
  - A directional speaker gives private audio in the listening zone, with minimal intelligible leakage outside it [EE, ME, AC]
  - EVT: masking sound outward from the workstation; acoustic side baffle (PRO2-156); joint deliverables (PRO2-434, PRO2-435) [ME, AC]
- **PRO2-3** Connectivity & Ports (due 2027-11-15, 5/16 done, variance −70d)
  - 24 V / 72 W DC adapter with PoE backup; USB-C (USB 3.2 + PD out), USB-A (5 V + debug), Gigabit Ethernet; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth [EE]
  - EVT-1 hardware Stories: power, USB, Ethernet/wireless, memory, AI accelerator, beamforming mic and speaker, touch, LED, cooling fan (PRO2-457 to PRO2-466) [EE]
- **PRO2-4** User Interface (Control and Status) (due 2027-11-15, 0/4 done, variance −280d)
  - One interface to control core functions and show status with clear feedback [EE, ME]
  - Touch controls, ring-light status, anti-theft tether, manual feedback UI (from child titles, inferred) [EE, ME]
- **PRO2-8** Mechanical Structure (Product Enclosure) (due 2027-11-15, 2/25 done, variance −70d)
  - House the i.MX 95 PCB, a 1.4 kg steel core and a 6 mm heat pipe in a 244 mm octagonal base with two 180 × 220 × 22 mm panels [ME, EE]
  - 76 cm 6-face drop (IEC 60068-2-31); IP30 (IEC 60529); stable at ≥10° desk incline [ME]
- **PRO2-172** PRO Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 5/6 done, variance −280d)
  - 6-step ID process, now at 5/6 Design Refinement (PRO2-304) [ID]
- **PRO2-449** Pro application development (due 2027-11-15, 16/22 done, variance −280d)
  - C++ TSE app (`libzenatse` + `zenatse`) on Raspberry Pi 5 and UCM-iMX95: ONNX Runtime, Delay-and-Sum/MVDR beamforming, MQTT, WebRTC [FW, SW (inferred)]
