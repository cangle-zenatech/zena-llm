## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Pro2

**Purpose:** A desk device for call-centre agents that gives private calls without a headset. An 8-mic array plus target speaker extraction (TSE) captures only the agent's voice for the customer. A directional ultrasonic speaker delivers the cleaned customer audio only to the agent's listening zone (PRO2-1, PRO2-2, PRO2-449).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- **PRO2-1** Layer 3 — Voice Lock: Inbound TSE (agent voice capture) (due 2027-09-06, 50% Stories done, variance −280d)
  - Capture target speech with an 8-mic array; suppress noise and competing speech [FW, EE]
  - Spatial filtering by MVDR beamforming to a 30°–150° sector, then TSE [FW]
  - 8-channel mic panel: FFC link, clock/sync, low-noise front end (from child titles) [EE]
  - EVT-1: mic port geometry/MEMS alignment, PCB vibration isolation, acoustic baffling [ME, AC]
- **PRO2-2** Layer 1 — Voice Lock: Outbound TSE (sound bubble) (due 2027-09-06, 43% done, variance −314d)
  - Clean incoming audio with the open-source Mask2Flow-TSE model [FW, AC]
  - Directional speaker: private audio to the agent, 120° front, sufficient volume at 1 m, adjustable angle [EE, ME]
  - 36-transducer speaker panel with ultrasonic driver/amplifier (from child titles) [EE]
  - EVT-1: acoustic side baffle for the speaker panel [ME]
