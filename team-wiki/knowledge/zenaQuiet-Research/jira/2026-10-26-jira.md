## 2. Research Detail

**Purpose:** The Acoustic team is working on three things. First, choosing a processor that can run the voice-isolation AI model ([[glossary#TSE|TSE]], target speech extraction) fast enough: under 150 ms one-way delay for a voice call ([[glossary#Latency|latency]]) (RS-1, RS-2). Second, "Layer 2: Office Acoustics" (RS-11): a mix of sound masking (a soft background noise that hides speech, RS-16), absorbing materials (RS-21), screens and seating (RS-23, RS-25), headset noise cancelling (ANC, active noise cancelling, RS-22) and before/after measurement (RS-24). Third, the ultrasonic (highly directional) loudspeaker: phased-array steering test RS-19, planned 2026-10-26 → 2026-11-30.

**Engineering discussion (from Jira comments):**

- **RS-1 Chip eval + decision** (comments on RS-1 and RS-2)
  - Being discussed: which chip can run the TSE model within the 150 ms one-way target for voice calls (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-12).
  - Thinking so far:
    - **i.MX95 (the chip on the paid POC boards):** the small model (9M parameters) takes 250 ms on CPU or on the NPU (the chip's built-in AI accelerator); 8-bit compression on the NPU degrades quality a lot. The larger AD-FlowTSE model runs 89% on the NPU but takes 1,500 ms in total (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22). A rewrite replacing LSTM layers with NPU-friendly transformer layers is in progress (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-12).
    - **Qualcomm:** on QCS8550 / IQ-8275 the small model runs in 37.7 ms (8-bit); AD-FlowTSE 148 ms (8-bit) to 350 ms (16-bit). Two options: fix the QCS6490 errors and use it, or use the IQ-8275 (temperature and price still to check; dev kit about 300 USD) (RS-1, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
    - **Hailo 8L:** benchmark of the small model planned (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
    - **Rockchip RK3576:** proposed by the hardware team (Ngoc). AD-FlowTSE compiles fully to its two NPU cores in simulation; about 600 ms per 3 s of audio on one core. Rated lower cost and easier to get than Qualcomm, whose NDA (non-disclosure agreement) is "too difficult". The author still prefers Qualcomm (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-23).
  - Decided: XMOS XU316 is out for the AI model: about 0.75 s to process 1 s of audio; kept as an option for other sound processing (RS-1, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
  - Still open: the chip choice itself; the RK3576 evaluation-kit purchase (no cost given; from comment); whether a Chinese chip is allowed. The comment says Sajjad now accepts Chinese chips, but [[reference#Sourcing rules]] still says "No components from China (CTO)". This is a conflict for the human to settle, not resolved here (waiting on Sajjad Asif, CTO, and Cang Le).

  - Being discussed: whether the Beacon masking demo could be ready by 2026-10-19 (RS-16, Cang Le, 2026-10-12).
  - Thinking so far: the Beacon board has no loudspeaker or microphone yet, and EE says it is still in design (RS-16, Huỳnh Thái Hòa, 2026-10-12); a Raspberry Pi could stand in for the demo (RS-16, Cang Le, 2026-10-14).
