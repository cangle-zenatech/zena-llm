- **RS-1 Chip eval + decision** (comments on RS-1 and RS-2)
  - Being discussed: which chip can run the TSE model within the 150 ms one-way target for voice calls.
  - Thinking so far:
    - **i.MX95 (the chip on the paid POC boards):** the small model (9M parameters) takes 250 ms on CPU or on the [[glossary#NPU|NPU]] (the chip's built-in AI accelerator); 8-bit compression on the NPU degrades quality a lot. The larger AD-FlowTSE model runs 89% on the NPU but takes 1,500 ms in total (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
    - **Qualcomm:** on QCS8550 / IQ-8275 the small model runs in 37.7 ms (8-bit); AD-FlowTSE 148 ms (8-bit) to 350 ms (16-bit). Two options: fix the QCS6490 errors and use it, or use the IQ-8275 (temperature and price still to check; dev kit about 300 USD) (RS-1, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
    - **Hailo 8L:** benchmark of the small model at 8-bit and 16-bit planned (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22); no result posted yet.
    - **Rockchip RK3576:** proposed by the hardware team (Ngoc). AD-FlowTSE compiles fully to its two NPU cores in simulation; about 600 ms per 3 s of audio on one core. Rated lower cost and easier to get than Qualcomm, whose NDA (non-disclosure agreement) is "too difficult". The author still prefers Qualcomm (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-23).
  - Decided: XMOS XU316 is out for the AI model: about 0.75 s to process 1 s of audio; kept as an option for other sound processing (RS-1, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
  - Still open: the chip choice itself; the RK3576 evaluation-kit purchase (no cost given; from comment); whether a Chinese chip is allowed. The comment says Sajjad now accepts Chinese chips, and at the 2026-10-15 meeting he said "For these products, you can purchase components from China, from Hong Kong" when asked about the ultrasonic part (`transcripts/2026-10-15-biweekly.md · [26:56]`). [[reference#Sourcing rules]] still says "No components from China (CTO)". This conflict is for the human to settle, not resolved here (waiting on Sajjad Asif, CTO, and Cang Le).
