## 2. Research Detail

**Purpose:** The Acoustic team is working on three things right now. First, a processor choice: can the voice-isolation AI model (TSE, target speech extraction) run fast enough on the NPU (AI accelerator) of the i.MX95 chip? (RS-1, RS-2, due 2026-10-16.) Second, "Layer 2: Office Acoustics": room-scale noise cancelling was found not to be feasible, so it is replaced by the standard open-office approach of absorb, block and cover (sound masking), with before-and-after measurements (RS-11).
