## 2. Research Detail

**Purpose:** The Acoustic team is working on three things right now. First, a processor choice: can the voice-isolation AI model (TSE, target speech extraction) run fast enough on the NPU (AI accelerator) of the i.MX95 chip? Today it takes about 0.45 s per 3 s of audio on 6 CPU cores (RS-1, RS-2, due 2026-10-16). Second, "Layer 2: Office Acoustics": room-scale noise cancelling was found not to be feasible, so it is replaced by the standard open-office approach of absorb, block and cover (sound masking), with before-and-after measurements (RS-11). Third, a survey of parts and layout for an ultrasonic (highly directional) loudspeaker, leading to a phased-array steering test once PCB version 2 is ready (RS-3, RS-4, RS-14, RS-19).

**Research by product:**

- **ZenaQuiet-Beacon (inferred)**
  - RS-11 Layer 2: Office Acoustics, through its Story RS-16: best practice for a sound-masking system in the Beacon. Notes so far: pink-like noise at about 40–45 dBA; a desk-level source may sound like a device and raise the noise the headset mics pick up, so do a listening test first.
- **ZenaQuiet-Headset (inferred)**
  - RS-8 Headset mic beamforming: empty Epic, no description or dates.
  - RS-11, through its Story RS-22 ANC in the personal headset: recommends an off-the-shelf ANC chip (Airoha, Bestechnic, Qualcomm). ANC helps against low-frequency HVAC rumble but not against nearby speech; ear-cup passive isolation handles that.
  - RS-1 Chip eval + decision: its title names no product, but its description says it is "Research on choosing Chip for Headset focus on NPU" (inferred; still counted as Unlinked under the rules).
- **ZenaQuiet-Pro2:** no item names or links Pro2. RS-4/RS-14 ultrasonic loudspeaker work fits Pro2's directional-speaker Voice Lock described in [[reference#Projects]] (inferred).
- **ZenaQuiet-Mask:** nothing found.
- **Product not stated:** RS-7 Drone propeller ANC, RS-9 Android mobile app, RS-10 Sound masking impl (all empty Epics); RS-15 Qt multi-unit app (2027-01-01 → 2027-03-31, RS-18 learning Qt now); RS-20 a TSE add-on that removes the known masking noise from the microphone signal, "to be integrated in EVT" if the research works.
