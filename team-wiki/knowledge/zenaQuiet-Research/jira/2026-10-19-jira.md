## 2. Research Detail

**Purpose:** The Acoustic team is working on three things. First, a processor choice: which chip can run the voice-isolation AI model (TSE, target speech extraction) fast enough for the Headset and Mask (RS-1, RS-2). The team measured the i.MX95 NPU (the chip's AI accelerator), and Minh will propose an XMOS chip on 2026-10-20. Second, "Layer 2: Office Acoustics" (RS-11). This is the standard open-office approach, a mix of methods instead of room-wide noise cancelling: cover (sound masking, a soft background noise that hides speech, RS-16), absorb (materials, RS-21), block (screens and seating, RS-23, RS-25), headset noise cancelling (RS-22) and before/after measurement (RS-24). Third, the ultrasonic (highly directional) loudspeaker: the component survey is done (RS-3), and a phased-array steering test is next (RS-19, from 2026-10-26).
