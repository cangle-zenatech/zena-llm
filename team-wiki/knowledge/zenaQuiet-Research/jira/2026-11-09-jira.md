- **RS-1 Chip eval + decision** (comments on RS-1 and RS-2)
  - Being discussed: which chip can run the TSE model within the 150 ms one-way target for voice calls.
  - Thinking so far:
    - **i.MX95 (the chip on the paid POC boards):** small model (9M parameters) 250 ms on CPU or on the [[glossary#NPU|NPU]] (built-in AI accelerator); 8-bit compression ([[glossary#Quantization|quantization]]) on the NPU degrades quality a lot. The larger AD-FlowTSE model takes 1,500 ms in total (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22). The Story closed Done on 2026-11-03 with no comment saying the i.MX95 is chosen or dropped.
    - **Qualcomm:** QCS8550 / IQ-8275 run the small model in 37.7 ms (8-bit) and AD-FlowTSE in 148 ms (8-bit) to 350 ms (16-bit). Two options: fix the QCS6490 errors, or use the IQ-8275 (temperature and price still to check; dev kit about 300 USD) (RS-1, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
    - **Rockchip RK3576:** proposed by the hardware team (Ngoc). AD-FlowTSE compiles fully to its two NPU cores in simulation; about 600 ms per 3 s of audio on one core. Rated cheaper and easier to get than Qualcomm, whose NDA (non-disclosure agreement) is "too difficult"; the author still prefers Qualcomm (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-23).
  - Decided:
    - XMOS XU316 is out for the AI model (about 0.75 s per 1 s of audio); kept for other sound processing (RS-1, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-10-22).
    - **New:** Hailo 8L is out: "Hailo 8L cannot fully convert the v49 model since architechture not suitable" (RS-2, Nguyen Binh Minh, 2026-11-03).
  - Still open: the chip choice itself (Qualcomm vs RK3576); whether the RK3576 kit is still wanted now that RS-2 is closed, and its cost (from comment); whether a Chinese chip is allowed. The RS-2 comment (2026-10-23) says Sajjad now accepts Chinese chips, and on 2026-10-15 he said "For these products, you can purchase components from China, from Hong Kong" about the ultrasonic part (`transcripts/2026-10-15-biweekly.md · [26:56]`). [[reference#Sourcing rules]] still says "No components from China (CTO)". Flagged for the human, not resolved here (waiting on Sajjad Asif, CTO, and Cang Le).
