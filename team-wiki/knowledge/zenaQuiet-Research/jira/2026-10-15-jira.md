## 2. Research Detail

**Purpose:** The Acoustic team is working on three things right now. First, a processor choice: can the voice-isolation AI model (TSE, target speech extraction) run fast enough on the NPU (the AI accelerator) of the i.MX95 chip, compared with about 0.45 s per 3 s of audio on 6 CPU cores today (RS-1, RS-2, due 2026-10-16). Second, "Layer 2: Office Acoustics": the standard open-office approach of absorb, block and cover (sound masking, i.e. a soft background noise that hides speech), with before-and-after measurements (RS-11). Third, a survey of parts and layout for an ultrasonic (highly directional) loudspeaker, leading to a phased-array steering test (RS-3, RS-4, RS-14, RS-19).

**Research by product:**

- **ZenaQuiet-Beacon (linked)**
  - RS-11 Layer 2: Office Acoustics, through its Story RS-16: best practice for a sound-masking system in the Beacon. Notes so far: professional systems play pink-like noise at about 40–45 dBA from the ceiling; a desk-level source may sound like a device and raise the noise the headset mics pick up, so do a listening test first. Linked to [BC-234](https://epazz.atlassian.net/browse/BC-234) "Sound Masketing" (Beacon Epic, To Do). The link is on the Story only, so the other RS-11 Stories stay Unlinked.

  - RS-1 Chip eval + decision: its title names no product, but its description says "Research on choosing Chip for Headset focus on NPU" (inferred; counted as Unlinked under the rules).
- **ZenaQuiet-Pro2:** no item names or links Pro2. The ultrasonic loudspeaker work (RS-4, RS-14) fits Pro2's directional-speaker Voice Lock described in [[reference#Projects]] (inferred).
