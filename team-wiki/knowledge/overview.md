# Projects Overview

**Portfolio (2026-10-14):** 4 of 4 products on track for 2027-11-15, all at low confidence (stages only 23 days old). Mask is the weakest: 17 POC Stories unowned and a pace that would finish POC about 193 days late. See [[portfolio/2026-10-14-rollup]].

One paragraph per active project: what it is and where it stands. Details are on each project's overview page and in its latest weekly report.

## ZenaQuiet-Pro2

A desk unit that keeps private call audio private without a headset ("Voice Lock"). It cleans up the customer's speech and plays it through a directional speaker, so only the agent hears it. **As of 2026-10-13:** in EVT, the first engineering-prototype stage (its stage review, called the gate, is on 2027-02-08, 118 days away). 28% of EVT work items are finished. Health AMBER. On 2026-10-12 every work item's dates were stretched to cover its whole stage, which hides early slips. See [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/overview]] and [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/jira/2026-10-13-jira|2026-10-13 report]].

## ZenaQuiet-Beacon

A ceiling unit, powered over the network cable, that coordinates a cluster of Pro units on a floor. It handles voice-consent rules, relays cloud commands and keeps zone settings in sync. **As of 2026-10-13:** in EVT, gate 2027-02-08 (118 days). 44% of EVT work items are finished, though almost all of them were finished firmware items logged in Jira afterwards, not new work. Health AMBER. On 2026-10-13 the cloud-connectivity part of the plan (Epic BC-106) and four unowned Pro-to-Beacon work items were removed from Jira, with no clear replacement yet. See [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/overview]] and [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/jira/2026-10-13-jira|2026-10-13 report]].

## ZenaQuiet-Headset

A call-centre headset with voice capture for Voice Lock, active noise cancelling and a detachable magnetic masking arc. **As of 2026-10-13:** in POC, the proof-of-concept stage (gate 2027-02-08, 118 days). 21% of POC work items are finished. Health AMBER, improved from RED on 2026-10-12, but only because the overdue item was given a later date, not finished. Mechanical work is moving; voice and noise-cancelling work has not started. See [[ZenaQuiet-Headset/overview]] and [[ZenaQuiet-Headset/jira/2026-10-13-jira|2026-10-13 report]].

Health AMBER. Most engineering work has no owner, and the dates were all reset to the whole stage on 2026-10-12.
