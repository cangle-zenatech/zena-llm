# Projects Overview

**Portfolio (2026-10-15):** 4 of 4 products on track for 2027-11-15, all at low confidence (stages only 24 days old), but no product has closed any work since 2026-10-13. ## ZenaQuiet-Pro2

A desk unit that keeps private call audio private without a headset ("Voice Lock"). It cleans up the customer's speech and plays it through a directional speaker, so only the agent hears it. **As of 2026-10-15:** in EVT, the first engineering-prototype stage (its stage review, called the gate, is on 2027-02-08, 116 days away). 28% of EVT work items are finished, unchanged since 2026-10-13; nothing in Pro2's Jira has changed since then. Health AMBER. The first work item is now overdue (PRO2-23). On 2026-10-12 every work item's dates were stretched to cover its whole stage, which hides early slips. See [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/overview]] and [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/jira/2026-10-15-jira|2026-10-15 report]].

## ZenaQuiet-Beacon

A ceiling unit, powered over the network cable, that coordinates a cluster of Pro units on a floor. It handles voice-consent rules, relays cloud commands and keeps zone settings in sync. **As of 2026-10-15:** in EVT, gate 2027-02-08 (116 days). 44% of EVT work items are finished, though almost all of them were finished firmware items logged in Jira afterwards. Health AMBER. On 2026-10-13 the cloud-connectivity part of the plan (Epic BC-106) and four unowned Pro-to-Beacon work items were removed from Jira and have not come back; the Pro-to-Beacon Epic now has an owner but no work items yet. A new sound-masking Epic (BC-234) was created on 2026-10-15. See [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/overview]] and [[ZenaQuiet-Beacon/jira/2026-10-15-jira|2026-10-15 report]].

## ZenaQuiet-Headset

No Jira changes since 2026-10-13. Health AMBER. Mechanical work is moving; voice and noise-cancelling work has not started, and most work has no owner. See [[ZenaQuiet-Headset/overview]] and [[ZenaQuiet-Headset/jira/2026-10-15-jira|2026-10-15 report]].
