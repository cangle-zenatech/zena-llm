# Projects overview

One paragraph per active project (see `CLAUDE.md` → Projects).

## [[ZenaQuiet-Pro2/overview|ZenaQuiet-Pro2]]

A desk device that gives call-centre agents private calls without a headset: it hears only the
agent's voice and plays the customer's voice only to the agent's seat. It is in EVT (engineering
tests), with the next stage starting 2027-02-08. Health on 2026-10-10 was AMBER: 1 of 39 EVT
Stories done and a lot of near-term work not yet started.

## [[ZenaQuiet-Mask/overview|ZenaQuiet-Mask]]

An everyday face mask that filters city air and keeps the wearer's conversations private from
people nearby, with the voice processing done on the paired phone. It is in POC (early proof of
concept) until 2027-02-08. The plan was set up in early October 2026; so far only Industrial Design
has started (2 of 38 tickets Done).

Not active: ZenaQuiet-Headset, ZenaQuiet-Beacon, ZenaQuiet-Research.
