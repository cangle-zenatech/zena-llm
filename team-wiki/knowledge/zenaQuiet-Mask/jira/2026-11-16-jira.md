## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A wearable powered face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to about KN95 level ([[glossary]]) using one small fan, and makes the wearer's voice 3–6 dB quieter to people nearby in the 1–4 kHz speech band without muffling it for calls (MK-6, MK-10). In Jira the voice processing (TSE, target speaker extraction, see [[glossary]]) runs on the paired phone and the mask has only a Bluetooth relay board (MK-13, MK-21); at the 2026-11-16 stand-up the TM said the POC will instead run the AI model on the mask (section 3).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- MK-1 MASK Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 50% done, variance -280d, To Do)
  - Styles for four age groups (10–14, 15–18, 19–25, 26–40), through a 6-step ID process. Step 3/6, Design Development (MK-4, 8 Subtasks), and step 4/6, Design Prototyping (MK-5, 7 Subtasks for the first mock-up), are both In Progress [ID]
  - Smallest shell that fits fan, filter, battery and board (MK-34, Done); shell plastics and skin-safe materials (MK-37, In Progress) [ID, ME, EE]
  - Detachable, washable fabric (MK-35, Done) and chin area (MK-38, Done); fabric ear band and chin cover for the mock-up (MK-42); S/M and M/L sizes from face data (MK-36, To Do) [ID, ME]
  - Mock-up tests: airflow, filtration and voice leakage (MK-45, MK-46, MK-47); battery, board and fan fitted by ID (MK-43) [ID, ME, EE (inferred)]
- MK-6 Air Filtration System (due 2027-11-15, 0% done, variance -280d, To Do)
  - Breathing resistance ≤86 Pa at target flow; KN95-class or lab-tested equivalent; certification in MK-9 [ME]
  - One off-the-shelf micro-blower fan (reference class 20×20×10 mm, 5 V, 20 dBA) drawing air from the face side, bench-tested in the mask's own duct (MK-8, In Progress) [ME]
  - Replaceable cartridge with about 30-day life (MK-7) [ME]

- MK-10 Sound Dampening & Voice Privacy
  - Being discussed: how to make the wearer's voice harder to understand for people nearby, and how to keep fan vibration from reaching the face. The proposal is a 7-layer stack (MK-11, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-10): outer and inner plastic housings, a barrier sheet bonded to the outer housing, a rubber ring between fan and housing, foam inside the mouth cavity, a sealed foam rim at the face, and foam in the air vents.
  - Thinking so far: speech clarity sits mostly at 2–5 kHz, where a plastic wall blocks best; a 2 mm wall blocks about 20 dB at 1 kHz and about 32 dB at 4 kHz by the "mass law" (heavier wall, more blocking), and sealing is the top priority (MK-11, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-10). A follow-up, posted as an AI-generated review, adds that leaks set the limit and that the two-shell air gap needs foam filling (MK-11, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-10).

He asked Acoustic (Huỳnh Thái Hòa) to advise on materials as part of the Acoustic team's "Layer 2" research, working with ID and ME (same comment).
