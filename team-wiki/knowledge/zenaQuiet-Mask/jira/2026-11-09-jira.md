## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A wearable face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to a KN95-class level with one small fan, and keeps the wearer's conversation private from people nearby (3–6 dB quieter in the 1–4 kHz band) without muffling their voice (MK-6, MK-10). In Jira, voice processing (TSE, target speaker extraction: isolating the wearer's voice, see [[glossary]]) runs fully on the paired phone, so the mask needs only a simple Bluetooth relay (MK-10, MK-13, MK-21). The meetings question this (section 3, processor row).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- MK-1 MASK Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 50% done, variance -280d, To Do)
  - Styles for four age groups (10–14, 15–18, 19–25, 26–40), via a 6-step ID process; 3/6 Design Development (MK-4) and 4/6 Design Prototyping (MK-5, 3D printing and ear-band material, started 2026-11-03) are both In Progress; 8 Subtasks, 6 Done [ID]
