## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A wearable face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to a KN95-class level using one small fan, and keeps the wearer's conversation private from people nearby (3–6 dB quieter in the 1–4 kHz band) without muffling their voice (MK-6, MK-10). In Jira, voice processing (TSE) runs fully on the paired phone, so the mask only needs a simple Bluetooth relay (MK-10, MK-13, MK-21). The meetings put this in question (section 3). The CTO approved continued research on 2026-09-30 and called the mask an optional add-on, not a required part of the office solution (`transcripts/2026-09-30-biweekly.md · [47:28]-[49:36]`).
