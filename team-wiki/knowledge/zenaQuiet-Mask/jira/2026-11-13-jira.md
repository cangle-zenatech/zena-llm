## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A wearable powered face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to about KN95 level ([[glossary]]) using one small fan. It also makes the wearer's voice 3–6 dB quieter to people nearby in the 1–4 kHz speech band, without muffling it for calls (MK-6, MK-10). In Jira the voice processing (TSE, target speaker extraction, see [[glossary]]) runs on the paired phone, so the mask needs only a simple Bluetooth relay board (MK-13, MK-21). The meetings still question this (section 3).

- MK-10 Sound Dampening & Voice Privacy
  - Being discussed: how to make the wearer's voice harder to understand for people nearby, and how to keep fan vibration from reaching the face. The proposal is a 7-layer stack (MK-11, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-10): outer and inner plastic housings, a barrier sheet bonded to the outer housing, a rubber ring between fan and housing, foam inside the mouth cavity, a sealed foam rim at the face, and foam in the air vents.
  - Thinking so far: speech clarity sits mostly at 2–5 kHz, where a plastic wall blocks best; a 2 mm wall blocks about 20 dB at 1 kHz and about 32 dB at 4 kHz by the "mass law", and sealing is the top priority (MK-11, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-10). A follow-up, posted as an AI-generated review, adds that leaks set the limit (1% open area cuts a 30 dB wall to about 20 dB), that felt in the vents gives only a few dB, and that the two-shell air gap needs foam filling because of a weak spot near 775 Hz (MK-11, Duy Nguyen, 2026-11-10).
  - Decided: nothing recorded. Three barrier materials are to be tested (silicone, rubber, MLV), choosing the lightest if results are close (attachment image).
  - Still open: which materials and layers to use, and how to baffle the vents without hurting airflow (MK-20, MK-8). No Subtasks or end date. Waiting on Duy Nguyen; Acoustic (Huỳnh Thái Hòa, MK-10) not mentioned.
