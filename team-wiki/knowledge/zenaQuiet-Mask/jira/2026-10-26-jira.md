## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A wearable face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to a KN95-class level with one small fan. It also keeps the wearer's conversation private from people nearby (3–6 dB quieter in the 1–4 kHz band) without muffling their voice (MK-6, MK-10). In Jira, voice processing (TSE, target speaker extraction: isolating the wearer's voice, see [[glossary]]) runs fully on the paired phone, so the mask needs only a simple Bluetooth relay (MK-10, MK-13, MK-21). The meetings question this (section 3, processor row).

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- MK-1 MASK Industrial Design (due 2027-11-15, 50% done, variance -280d, To Do)
  - Styles for four age groups (10–14, 15–18, 19–25, 26–40), via a 6-step ID process; now at 3/6 Design Development (MK-4) with 8 Subtasks, 3 Done [ID]
  - Smallest, lightest shell that fits fan, filter, battery and board, balanced on nose and ears (MK-34, Done) [ID, ME, EE]
  - Detachable, washable fabric; S/M and M/L sizes; chin area that seals the filter (MK-35, MK-36, MK-38) [ID, ME]
