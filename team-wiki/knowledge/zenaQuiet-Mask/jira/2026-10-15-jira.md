## 2. Product Detail

**Purpose:** A wearable face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to a KN95-class level with one small fan and keeps the wearer's conversation private from people nearby (3–6 dB quieter in the 1–4 kHz band) without muffling their voice (MK-6, MK-10). Voice processing (TSE) runs fully on the paired smartphone, so the mask only needs a simple Bluetooth relay (MK-10, MK-21). Epic descriptions are unchanged from last week.
