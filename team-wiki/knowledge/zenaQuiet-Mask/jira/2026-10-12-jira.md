## 2. Product Detail

### ZenaQuiet-Mask

**Purpose:** A wearable face mask for office workers and commuters. It filters city air to a KN95-class level with a small fan and keeps the wearer's conversation private from people nearby (3–6 dB quieter in the 1–4 kHz band) without muffling their voice (MK-6, MK-10). Voice processing (TSE) runs on the paired smartphone, so the mask itself only needs a simple Bluetooth relay (MK-10, MK-21). MK-2 records the two main features as "air filtration" and "Inbound Voice Lock".

**Technical requirements by Epic:**

- **MK-1** MASK Industrial Design (due 2026-11-30, 17% Stories done, variance 0d)
  - Style for four age groups (10–14, 15–18, 19–25, 26–40); 6-step ID process, now at 3/6 Design Development (MK-4) [ID]
  - Smallest, lightest shell that fits fan, filter, battery and PCB, balanced on nose and ears (MK-34) [ID, ME, EE]
  - Detachable washable fabric; two sizes S/M and M/L from NIOSH fit-panel data; chin coverage for the filter seal (MK-35, MK-36, MK-38) [ID, ME]
  - Battery: certified cells, protection circuit, flame-retardant housing, skin temperature limit; PCB protected from breath moisture (MK-39, MK-40) [ID, EE]
- **MK-6** Air Filtration System (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance n/a, no owner)
  - Breathing resistance ≤86 Pa at target flow; KN95-class or lab-tested equivalent, no NIOSH claim [ME]
  - Twin side-mount, tool-free replaceable cartridge, ≥30-day filter life (MK-7) [ME]
  - One sourced micro-blower fan, tuned for quiet low-speed running (MK-8) [ME, EE (inferred)]
- **MK-10** Sound Dampening & Voice Privacy (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance n/a)
  - 3–6 dB outward voice dampening, 1–4 kHz, through the seal's laminate (MK-11) [ME, AC (inferred)]
  - One MEMS mic at the nose bridge, no fan noise in the voice band, no muffled own-voice (MK-12) [ME, AC (inferred)]
  - Bluetooth pairing/reconnection; TSE 100% on the phone, no on-device NPU; USB-C (MK-13) [FW, ME, SW]
- **MK-14** Mechanical Comfort & Wearability (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance n/a, no owner)
  - Total weight 60–80 g; comfortable past 4–6 hours of wear [ME]
  - Back-of-head dual strap, not ear loops; jaw clearance through speech (MK-16, MK-17) [ME]
- **MK-19** Exhale System (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance n/a, no owner)
  - Exhale vent must not become the main sound-leak path; baffled/labyrinth geometry, low exhale resistance (MK-20) [ME, AC (inferred)]
- **MK-21** Electronics & Power (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance n/a)
  - Power budget for fan, mic, BT relay and LED at worst-case draw; BOM target about $18–22 [EE]
  - BT audio relay SoC, USB-C charging, thermal check (MK-22) [EE]
  - Single Li-Po cell sized for full-day fan + BT + mic load (MK-23) [EE]
- **MK-24** Product Line Rollout (due 2027-09-06, 0% done, variance n/a, no owner)
  - Standard (office) variant validates the whole architecture first (MK-25) [ME]
  - Lifestyle, Sport and eSport variants follow and are marked "Deferred"; only Sport changes internals (MK-26, MK-27, MK-28) [ME]
- **MK-29** Certification & Compliance (2027-09-06 → 2027-11-15, 0% done, variance n/a, no owner)
  - Battery passes UN 38.3 and IEC 62133 before shipment (MK-30) [EE]
  - Every filtration/dampening claim traceable to test data (MK-31) [all teams]
- **MK-32** Field Test+RC (2027-02-08 → 2027-05-24, no Stories)
